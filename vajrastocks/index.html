VajraStocks — NSE Quantitative Stock Research & Screening Platform

Free, local-first quantitative research and screening platform tracking 2,365+ Indian equities on the National Stock Exchange (NSE). Features sub-5ms screener queries, 4 advanced chart types, LightGBM ML models, and a 7-agent DAG running entirely on local Ollama LLMs.

Core Subsystems

  • Explorer Dashboard: Candlestick, Heikin-Ashi, Renko ATR, and Three Line Break charts with automated Trade Plan calculations.
  • Sub-5ms Screener: Denormalized EOD snapshot cache scanning 2,365+ stocks across 12 preset quant scans.
  • 7-Agent AI DAG: Orchestrator, SQL Specialist, Market Regime, Trade Planner, Backtester, Quant Researcher, and Report Writer running locally via Ollama.
  • Machine Learning: LightGBM walk-forward validation across 489,296 rows of NSE market history.
