# Feature Specification: v1.2.0 Comprehensive SEO, AEO & GEO Strategy

- **Author:** Abhishek Kumar & Antigravity

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This major update transforms `abhishek4official.github.io` from a single-page portfolio into an authoritative, multi-hub technical publication and project showcase optimized for:
1. **Traditional Search Engines:** Google Search & Bing Search.
2. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO):** ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot.
3. **Machine-Readable AI Standards:** Full compliance with `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` (per llmstxt.org).

---

## 2. Information Architecture & Topical Hubs
The site preserves all legacy URLs (`/` and `/vajrastocks/`) while adding dedicated, crawlable technical hubs:
- `/ai-agents/`: Autonomous AI Agents, multi-agent architectures, and the deterministic 5-Phase Strict Gated Coding Pipeline (`CodingAgent`).
- `/mcp/`: Model Context Protocol (MCP) developer guide, client-server mechanics, Python SDK implementations, and MCP vs Function Calling.
- `/agent-frameworks/`: Production comparison of Microsoft Agent Framework (Semantic Kernel + AutoGen unification), LangGraph, and native DAG pipelines.
- `/local-ai/`: Local AI systems, Ollama integration, zero-cloud architectures, and the VajraStocks 7-agent DAG case study.

---

## 3. Machine-Readable & Structured Data Standards
1. **`llms.txt` & `llms-full.txt`**: Deployed at root to provide large language models with concise, high-signal markdown summaries and direct links.
2. **Schema.org Knowledge Graph**:
   - `Person` & `ProfilePage` for Abhishek Kumar with verified `sameAs` links.
   - `TechArticle` for all technical guides and architecture breakdowns.
   - `SoftwareSourceCode` and `SoftwareApplication` for `CodingAgent` and `VajraStocks`.
   - `BreadcrumbList` for structured navigation.
3. **AI Crawler Policies in `robots.txt`**: Explicitly permits AI search crawlers (`OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `PerplexityBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`).
4. **Dynamic `sitemap.xml`**: Lists all 6 primary routes with priority and update timestamps.

---

## 4. Dual-Layer Delivery for GitHub Pages
- **Interactive SPA (React 19 + Vite):** Smooth, client-side transitions, interactive chart demos, and filterable skill matrices.
- **Static Pre-Rendering (`postbuild.js`):** Automatically generates physical directories (`/ai-agents/index.html`, `/mcp/index.html`, etc.) containing pre-rendered semantic HTML, ensuring search and AI bots receive complete text without executing JavaScript.
